# Captions for tables produced outside make_tables.py

These tables come from the analysis scripts named in the last column. `make_tables.py` rewrites `README.md` from its own
table set, so their captions live here and are appended to `README.md` by that script and read by the manuscript builders.

| T13_holdout_final | Unseen-frame hold-out (2,000 rows from 32 sentence frames in six families, built after model selection and evaluated once).
